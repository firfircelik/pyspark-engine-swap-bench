## F4 characterization

F4 here is the intermittent Spark execution-memory failure `UNABLE_TO_ACQUIRE_MEMORY` / `Unable to acquire 65536 bytes of memory, got 0` (SQLSTATE 53200), with stack `UnsafeExternalSorter.growPointerArrayIfNecessary` → `UnsafeExternalRowSorter.insertRow`. **This is characterized as a configuration limit under this matrix (Spark `local[8]`, driver 6g, cgroup memory.max 9 GiB), not as a Spark or AAL bug**, unless later evidence clearly shows otherwise. The same cell often succeeds on retry without changing memory settings; `spark.memory.fraction` and `spark.sql.shuffle.partitions` were not varied in this study, so their effect is not measured. No upstream issue was filed.

A second, distinct phenomenon is also recorded here because it dominated wall-clock waste: **no-progress stalls**, mostly on Spark classic, where a job writes `query_start` and then makes no further query progress for a long time (CPU nearly idle). Some of these ran for many hours until a timeout fired (the 90-minute `run_one.py` timeout sometimes did not trip until the box unfroze, or `cli.wait()` hung after kill). From 2026-11-25 23:32 Europe/Istanbul, remaining aal-iso jobs used a 30-minute job timeout and a 20-minute no-`queries.jsonl`-progress stall kill (`STALLED_NO_PROGRESS`). Long RSS-sampler gaps on queries that still completed are listed separately (timing contaminated; those attempts were marked frozen and rerun).

**F4 OOM count: 17** (Phase 2: 7, including dropped rep 3; AAL isolation: 10). Every one of these errors is the same 65536-byte acquisition failure.
